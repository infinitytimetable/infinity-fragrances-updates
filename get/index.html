Infinity Fragrances

The app for our resellers, shopkeepers and managers

Android phone Your phone

Samsung, Tecno, Itel, Infinix, Huawei and other Android phones · 4.8 MB

Download the app
  1. Tap Download the app and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file (from the notification or the Files / Downloads app).
  3. If your phone says the app is from an unknown source, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Sign in under Team login with the Login ID and PIN you were sent.

After this the app updates itself. You never need to download it again.

iPhone Your phone

Works from Safari, no App Store needed

Open the iPhone app
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Open the iPhone app.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Infinity from your home screen.
  5. Sign in under Team login with the Login ID and PIN you were sent.

Windows PC Your computer

For the admin and office computers · Windows 10 or 11 · about 105 MB · version 2.23.0

Download for Windows
  1. Tap Download for Windows and wait for the zip file to finish.
  2. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All, then open the folder Infinity Fragrances Hub.
  3. Double-click Infinity Fragrances Hub.exe. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. Click Install (or Update if an older version is already on the computer). Your records are kept.
  5. Sign in with your admin email, or under Team login with your Login ID and PIN.

After this the app updates itself. Open READ ME FIRST.txt in the folder for details.
