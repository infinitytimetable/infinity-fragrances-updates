INFINITY FRAGRANCES HUB __VER__ - PC VERSION (Windows 10 / 11, 64-bit)
=====================================================================

NEW INSTALL
1. Right-click the zip file and choose "Extract All...".
2. Open the extracted folder "Infinity Fragrances Hub".
3. Double-click "Infinity Fragrances Hub.exe".
   If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
   (This appears because the program is not signed with a paid certificate.)
4. Click "Install". The hub copies itself to this computer and adds icons to the
   desktop and Start menu. You can then delete the downloaded zip and folder.

UPDATING FROM AN OLDER VERSION (your records are kept)
1. Open your current hub and go to Settings & backup, then download a full backup
   (or "Back up to flash drive"). This is only for safety.
2. Close the hub completely.
3. Extract this zip and double-click "Infinity Fragrances Hub.exe" as above.
4. It says "Update Infinity Fragrances Hub from <old> to <new>?". Click "Update".
   The new hub opens and the desktop and Start menu icons now open the new version.
   If you still see an old icon next to the new one, delete the old one.
5. After this, later updates arrive by themselves (Reports & settings, Updates)
   and you will not need to download the zip again, unless a message tells you the app
   itself has changed.

WHERE YOUR RECORDS ARE
- Documents\Infinity Fragrances Hub\data.json   (your records)
- Documents\Infinity Fragrances Hub\backups\    (automatic backups, last 30 kept)
- Settings & backup > "Back up to flash drive" copies your records anywhere you choose.

SIGN-IN
- Admin: sign in with your admin email and password. Managers use their own Team login.
- Online records, orders, the shop, riders, staff and approvals all update by themselves
  when the computer is online.

Smell good. Live infinitely.
